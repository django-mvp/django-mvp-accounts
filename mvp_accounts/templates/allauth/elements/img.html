{% comment %} The two-factor QR code, and any other image allauth draws. allauth's QR code is dark modules on a transparent canvas. On a dark theme those sit on a dark page and cannot be scanned, so the image is always drawn on white, with padding for the quiet zone the scanner needs. `bg-white` is the fixed colour, not a theme colour, so it is white in every theme. `src` and `alt` go through `{{ }}` so they are escaped. `alt` is left out when allauth does not give one, because an empty `alt` would tell a screen reader the image is decorative. {% endcomment %}
